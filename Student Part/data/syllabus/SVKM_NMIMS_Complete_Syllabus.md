## COURSE 1: Cyber Security
**Course Code:** 702AI0E004
**Program:** B Tech (Computer Engineering, Computer Science, AI and ML), MBA Tech (Computer Engineering), B Tech Integrated (Computer Engineering)
**Semester:** V / VI / VII / X
**Credits:** 3
**Teaching Scheme:** 2 hrs Lecture + 2 hrs Practical per week
**Evaluation:** ICA – Marks Scaled to 50 | TEE – Marks Scaled to 50
**Prerequisite:** Computer Programming

### Course Objective
Introduction to the field of Cyber Security, balancing managerial and technical aspects. Prepares students with technical knowledge and skills needed to protect and defend computer systems and networks.

### Course Outcomes
1. Explain the basics of cyber security
2. Implement mechanisms of cryptography, authentication and access controls
3. Differentiate security mechanisms in programs and networks
4. Describe risk management related to computer security

### Detailed Syllabus

| Unit | Topic | Duration (hrs) |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | **Introduction** – Basic components of computer security (CIA), characteristics of information, vulnerabilities, threats, attacks and controls, classifications of hackers | 3 |
| 2 | **Cryptography** – Cryptographic basics, transposition cipher, substitution cipher, block and stream cipher steganography, public vs private key encryption, Private key encryption: DES, Public key encryption: RSA, Key management, Key exchange – Diffie-Hellman, Digital Signature, one-way hash functions | 7 |
| 3 | **Authentication** – Authentication basics, Password, Challenge response, Biometrics | 3 |
| 4 | **Access Control** – Access control principles, ACL, DAC, MAC, and Role based Access Control, Access control models, Kerberos | 3 |
| 5 | **Program Security** – Secure programs, non-malicious Program Errors, Viruses and other malicious code, types of viruses, attack mechanism of viruses, Targeted Malicious Code, Controls Against Program Threats | 4 |
| 6 | **Network Security** – Eavesdropping, spoofing, denial of service attacks, Security controls: encryption, virtual private networks, SSL, Firewall: Kinds of Firewalls, Filtering Services, DMZ, IDS and its types | 6 |
| 7 | **Risk Management** – Risk analysis, various terminologies associated with risk management, Risk assessment techniques, managing risk, steps for risk management, Business impact analysis, various terminologies associated with BIA, Different types of continuity planning, testing and revising the plan | 4 |
| | **Total** | **30** |

### Text Books

2. M. Whitman, H. Mattford, *Principles of Information Security*, 6th Edition, Cengage Learning, 2017
3. C. Pfleeger, S. Pfleeger, *Security in Computing*, 5th Edition, Pearson Education, 2015

### Reference Books

## COURSE 4: Computer Vision
**Course Code:** 702EX0E015
**Program:** B Tech (Computer Engineering, Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, CSE (DS) 311), MBA Tech (Computer Engineering), B Tech Integrated (Computer Engineering, Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering)
**Semester:** V / VI / VII / VIII / X
**Credits:** 3
**Teaching Scheme:** 2 hrs Lecture + 2 hrs Practical per week
**Evaluation:** ICA – Marks Scaled to 50 | TEE – Marks Scaled to 50
**Prerequisite:** Programming concepts and Image Processing fundamentals

### Course Objective
To provide concepts of Computer Vision, key algorithms for image descriptors, image segmentation, and motion estimation. Covers emerging object detection techniques using deep learning.

### Course Outcomes
1. Explain fundamental image processing techniques required for computer vision (L2 – Understanding)
2. Apply feature extraction and image segmentation techniques (L3 – Applying)
3. Understand the concept of image classification and image recognition (L2 – Understanding)
4. Analyze applications of computer vision techniques in real-time scenarios (L4 – Analyzing)
5. Implement computer vision techniques using appropriate tools and frameworks (L3 – Applying)

### Detailed Syllabus

| Unit | Topic | Duration (hrs) |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | **Introduction to Computer Vision and Image Descriptors** – Introduction to Computer Vision, Low-level, Mid-level, High-level, Overview of Diverse Computer Vision Applications. Edge Detectors: Laplacian Of Gaussian (LOG), Difference Of Gaussians (DOG), Histogram of Oriented Gaussian | 4 |
| 2 | **Interest Point Descriptors** – Harris, Hessian, Local invariant feature detectors and descriptors, SIFT, SURF, FAST, BRIEF, ORB | 5 |
| 3 | **Feature Matching** – Matching key points, weighted distance function, normalized cross correlation, cosine similarity, matching criteria, matching histograms, Geometric transformation, correspondence and least square, RANSAC, From points to images, nearest neighbor matching, bag of words, Vector of Locally Aggregated Descriptors (VLAD) representation and matching, pyramid matching – Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) | 6 |

| 5 | **Stereo Vision and Camera Calibration** – Overview, Linear camera model, intrinsic and extrinsic matrices, simple stereo, Image transformations, 2D Transformation, Geometric Operators, Affine Transformations, Computing Homography, dealing with outliers, warping and blending images, Epipolar geometry, Stereo Vision for depth estimation | 4 |
| 6 | **Object Detection using Deep Learning** – Review of Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), Region-based Convolutional Neural Network (R-CNN), Fast RCNN, Faster RCNN, You Only Look Once (YOLO) | 6 |
| | **Total** | **30** |
